These are the lecture notes for AAE 25100 Introduction to Aerospace Design in the School of Aeronautics and Astronautics at Purdue University. The instructor is Prof. Thiago Guimaraes.

The notes have five chapters, one for each group of course standards. Each chapter has one page per standard: the page states the standard, explains the ideas and the methods behind it, and ends with a short quiz you can use to check yourself. The quiz is not graded and nothing is recorded. A chapter may also have a page on material that no standard assesses; that page has no quiz.
